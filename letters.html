Advising policy · Revised September 2026

Before you ask me for a letter

A recommendation is testimony, not a formality. These are the conditions under which I can give it.

Sarvesh Pandey · Assistant Professor, Computer Science
MMV, Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi
sarveshpandey@bhu.ac.in

Eligibility

PhD · Master’s

A thesis or dissertation submitted under my guidance, with clear deliverables.

Postdoc · Researcher

At least one project taken through to article submission.

Undergraduate

A minimum three-month internship with me, with clear deliverables.

Minimum notice

2 weeksAny new request, measured from your earliest deadline
1 weekAdditions to a cycle I have already agreed to

Terms

  1. Prior consent. Take my consent before putting my name down as a referee — for each application, each cycle.
  2. Candid assessment. I write only what I have observed, and I will tell you privately if the letter would be a weak one.
  3. Confidentiality. The letter goes directly to the institution, never to you — before or after the cycle.
  4. One reminder. A nudge near the deadline is welcome; I would rather have it than miss the date.
  5. Right of refusal. I may decline at any point before submission, without stating a reason.

Send in one email

  • CV
  • Statement of purpose or cover letter, near-final
  • Full programme list, with deadlines and submission method
  • The work itself
  • One page: dates, contributions, points to emphasise

If you don’t meet the threshold yet: go to someone you have actually worked with — or come and work with me.

Ask first, ask early, and give me something true to say. sarveshpandey@bhu.ac.in · subject line Reference request — [Your name] — [Earliest deadline]
